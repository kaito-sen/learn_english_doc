# Verb Tenses

English verbs have **12 tenses**, formed by combining three *times* (past,
present, future) with four *aspects* (simple, continuous, perfect, perfect
continuous).

## Overview

| | Simple | Continuous | Perfect | Perfect Continuous |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Past** | I worked | I was working | I had worked | I had been working |
| **Present** | I work | I am working | I have worked | I have been working |
| **Future** | I will work | I will be working | I will have worked | I will have been working |

---

## Present Simple

**Form:** `subject + base verb` (*-s/-es* for he/she/it)

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Affirmative | She **works** in a hospital. |
| Negative | She **does not work** on Sundays. |
| Question | **Does** she **work** at night? |

**Uses:** habits and routines, general truths, schedules.

- Water **boils** at 100 °C. *(general truth)*
- The train **leaves** at 7:30. *(schedule)*

::: warning Common mistake
Do not forget the **-s** in third person singular:
❌ *He work here.* → ✅ *He work**s** here.*
:::

## Present Continuous

**Form:** `am/is/are + verb-ing`

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Affirmative | They **are studying** now. |
| Negative | They **are not studying** tonight. |
| Question | **Are** they **studying**? |

**Uses:** actions happening now, temporary situations, planned future
arrangements.

- I **am meeting** the manager tomorrow. *(arrangement)*

::: tip Stative verbs
Verbs of states — *know, like, believe, own, want* — are normally **not** used
in continuous forms:
❌ *I am knowing the answer.* → ✅ *I know the answer.*
:::

## Past Simple

**Form:** `verb-ed` (irregular verbs have special forms: *go → went*)

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Affirmative | We **visited** Hoi An last year. |
| Negative | We **did not visit** Da Nang. |
| Question | **Did** you **visit** the old town? |

**Uses:** completed actions at a specific past time.

- She **graduated** in 2020.

## Past Continuous

**Form:** `was/were + verb-ing`

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Affirmative | I **was reading** when he called. |
| Negative | I **was not reading** at midnight. |
| Question | **Were** you **reading**? |

**Uses:** actions in progress at a past moment; background actions
interrupted by a shorter one.

- I **was cooking** when the phone **rang**.

## Present Perfect

**Form:** `have/has + past participle`

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Affirmative | He **has finished** the report. |
| Negative | He **has not finished** it yet. |
| Question | **Has** he **finished** it? |

**Uses:** past actions with present results, experiences, unfinished time
periods (*today, this week*).

- I **have lived** here for ten years. *(and still do)*
- **Have** you ever **tried** sushi? *(experience)*

::: warning Present perfect vs. past simple
Use the **past simple** with finished time expressions:
❌ *I have seen him yesterday.* → ✅ *I **saw** him yesterday.*
:::

## Future Simple

**Form:** `will + base verb`

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Affirmative | It **will rain** tonight. |
| Negative | It **will not (won't) rain** tonight. |
| Question | **Will** it **rain** tonight? |

**Uses:** predictions, spontaneous decisions, promises.

::: tip *will* vs. *going to*
- **will** → predictions, instant decisions: *I'll help you!*
- **be going to** → plans and intentions: *We are going to move next month.*
:::

## Perfect and Perfect Continuous Tenses

| Tense | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Past Perfect | `had + V3` | The film **had started** when we arrived. |
| Future Perfect | `will have + V3` | By June, I **will have graduated**. |
| Present Perfect Continuous | `have/has been + V-ing` | I **have been waiting** for an hour. |
| Past Perfect Continuous | `had been + V-ing` | She **had been working** there for years. |
| Future Perfect Continuous | `will have been + V-ing` | In May, he **will have been teaching** for 20 years. |
| Future Continuous | `will be + V-ing` | This time tomorrow, I **will be flying** to Tokyo. |

::: details Quick self-check
1. By next week, they ___ (finish) the project.
2. While I ___ (walk) home, I saw an accident.
3. She ___ (learn) English since 2022.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. will **have finished** *(future perfect)*
2. **was walking** *(past continuous)*
3. **has been learning** *(present perfect continuous)*
</details>
:::
