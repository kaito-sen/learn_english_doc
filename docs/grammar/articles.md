# Articles: a, an, the

English has two articles — the **indefinite** (*a/an*) and the **definite**
(*the*) — plus the **zero article** (no article at all).

## Indefinite Article: a / an

Use *a/an* with **singular countable nouns** when the listener does not know
exactly which one you mean.

- She bought **a** car. *(any car)*
- I saw **an** eagle. *(one of many)*

**a vs. an** depends on **sound**, not spelling:

| Article | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | before a consonant *sound* | **a** book, **a** university, **a** European city |
| **an** | before a vowel *sound* | **an** apple, **an** hour, **an** MBA |

::: warning Common mistakes
- ❌ *a hour* → ✅ **an** hour *(the *h* is silent)*
- ❌ *an university* → ✅ **a** university *(sounds like "yoo")*
- ❌ *I have a money.* → ✅ *I have **some** money.* *(uncountable noun)*
:::

## Definite Article: the

Use *the* when both speaker and listener know **which specific thing** is
meant.

- **The** car in front of us is speeding. *(we both see it)*
- Close **the** door, please. *(a specific door)*

### Main uses of *the*

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Already mentioned | I bought a laptop. **The** laptop is fast. |
| Unique things | **the** sun, **the** internet, **the** government |
| Superlatives & ordinals | **the** best film, **the** first day |
| Inventions & musical instruments | **the** telephone, play **the** piano |
| Families (plural names) | **the** Smiths |
| Some countries with union/republic | **the** United States, **the** Netherlands |

## Zero Article (no article)

Use **no article** with:

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Plural & uncountable nouns in general | **Dogs** are loyal. **Water** boils at 100 °C. |
| Most proper nouns | **Hanoi**, **Mary**, **Asia** |
| Meals | after **breakfast**, have **lunch** |
| Languages & school subjects | She speaks **English**. I study **physics**. |
| Sports | play **football** |

::: tip Quick test
Ask yourself: **"Do we both know exactly which one?"**

- Yes → use **the**
- No, singular countable → use **a/an**
- No, plural or uncountable → use **no article**
:::

## Practice

::: details Fill in a, an, the, or — (no article)
1. I need ___ umbrella. It's raining.
2. ___ Moon goes around ___ Earth.
3. She is ___ honest person.
4. We had ___ dinner at ___ nice restaurant.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. **an** umbrella
2. **The** Moon, **the** Earth
3. **an** honest person *(silent h)*
4. **—** dinner (meals), **a** nice restaurant
</details>
:::
