# Sentence Structure

Every English sentence is built from **clauses** — groups of words containing
a subject and a verb. How clauses combine determines the sentence type.

## Clauses

| Type | Contains | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent** | complete thought, can stand alone | *She reads every night.* |
| **Dependent (subordinate)** | cannot stand alone, starts with a subordinator | *…because she loves stories.* |

Common subordinators: *because, although, when, if, while, since, after,
unless, that, who, which*.

## The Four Sentence Types

### 1. Simple Sentence

**One** independent clause.

- My brother **plays football** every weekend.

A simple sentence can still have compound subjects or verbs:

- Tom and Jerry **quarrel and chase** each other.

### 2. Compound Sentence

**Two or more** independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction
(**FANBOYS**: *for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so*) or a semicolon.

- I called her twice, **but** she didn't answer.
- The bus was full**;** we took a taxi instead.

::: warning Comma rule
When joining two independent clauses with a FANBOYS word, put a **comma
before** the conjunction:
❌ *I was tired but I kept working.* → ✅ *I was tired**,** but I kept working.*
:::

### 3. Complex Sentence

**One** independent clause + **at least one** dependent clause.

- **Although it was raining**, we went hiking.
- We went hiking **although it was raining**.

::: tip Comma with dependent clauses
Use a comma only when the dependent clause comes **first**:
- ✅ *Because he was ill, he stayed home.*
- ✅ *He stayed home because he was ill.* *(no comma)*
:::

### 4. Compound-Complex Sentence

**Two or more** independent clauses + **at least one** dependent clause.

- **When the show ended**, people applauded, **and** the actors took a bow.

## Common Errors

| Error | Wrong | Correct |
|---|---|---|
| Sentence fragment | *Because I was tired.* | *I went to bed **because I was tired**.* |
| Run-on sentence | *It was late we went home.* | *It was late**, so** we went home.* |
| Comma splice | *She left early, she felt sick.* | *She left early**because** she felt sick.* |

::: details Self-check — identify the sentence type
1. The sun rises in the east.
2. He studied hard, so he passed the exam.
3. If you heat ice, it melts.
4. While I cooked, my sister cleaned, and my brother set the table.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. **Simple** *(one independent clause)*
2. **Compound** *(two independent clauses with "so")*
3. **Complex** *(dependent "If…" + independent clause)*
4. **Compound-complex** *(dependent clause + two independent clauses)*
</details>
:::
